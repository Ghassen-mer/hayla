Origin and roast

Beans that travelled the frankincense road.

Natural and washed lots from the highlands of Yemen and Ethiopia, roasted in small batches in Muscat every week. Medium for espresso, light for kahwa, never more than fourteen days off roast.

The grind

Ground the moment you order.

Brass burrs turn the beans to a fine, even grind seconds before your shot is pulled. Coffee loses its perfume fast, so we never grind ahead.

The brew

Nine bars. Twenty‑eight seconds.

The basket locks in, water at 93°C meets the coffee, and steady pressure draws out the sweetness. One shot at a time, tasted every morning before we open.

The pour

Poured slowly, the way a host fills a finjan.

Dark coffee first, then the crema rising gold on top. In Oman you never rush a guest's cup, and we won't rush yours.

The cup

Hot, ready, and served with dates.

Every cup at Hayla arrives on a brass tray with two Bahlawi dates. Stay as long as you like. Kahwa refills are on us.

A majlis for coffee people.

Hayla began with one idea: serve specialty coffee the way Omani homes serve kahwa. Generously, without hurry, and always with something sweet on the side.

We roast in small batches, buy from farms we know by name, and season our drinks with the flavors we grew up with. The room is quiet, the cups are warm, and nobody will ask you to leave.
